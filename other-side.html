THE WRITTEN WORD

A few words from the heart.

01 / REFLECTION

The One That Never Was

I am but a small voice.
I am but a small dream.
A child with red bananas,
A student with big plans,
A teen who believed in forever,
A young man who dared to dream again.

I was the boy who wanted to be an attorney,
The friend who never let go,
The lover who gave too much,
The dreamer who saw a brighter tomorrow.

But life had its own plans,
And the road was never easy.
I lost some battles,
I missed some chances,
And I became someone
I never imagined I would be.

So here I am—
A collection of hopes and heartbreaks,
Of beautiful scars and silent battles,
Of dreams that lived on,
Even when they never came true.

Because maybe,
The one that never was…
is not a person,
but a part of me—
the dreams I carried,
the lessons I learned,
and the strength I found
in never giving up.

And in the end…
I am still here,
still dreaming.

02 / ROMANCE

A Moment to Treasure

Baby, thank you for being pure and true,
For bringing colors beyond the blue.

I knew there was something special in you,
From the very first moment, did you feel it too?

The timely smiles, the joy you bring,
Can lift my heart and make me sing.

You walked me gently back into the light,
And showed me once more that life was bright.

I wish that time would cease to flow,
And keep us here, with nowhere to go.

Wait, stop! A time spell I shall cast,
Oh, how I wish this moment could last!

These precious moments I will treasure,
And keep them safe beyond all measure.

Hey, baby! May I hold your hand?
And make forever our own little land?

03 / COMPANIONSHIP

Me and You

Me and you;

Through thick and thin,
through every failure and win.

We do what we do.

We fight and make up.
Out of the blue,
we learn as we go.

In what is there to come,
we might have none or some.

There is one thing sure.
The love we have will endure.
